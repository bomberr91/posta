# SambaNova Cloud Free Tier: Registration Requirements, Credit Card Policy, and Rate Limits

**Date of Research:** November 1, 2026
**Target Audience:** Developers and researchers evaluating the SambaNova platform

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The SambaNova Cloud platform, built on SambaNova's proprietary Reconfigurable Dataflow Units (RDUs) rather than conventional GPUs, offers developers access to large language models through a tiered pricing structure. As of November 2026, the platform has undergone a significant transition: the original no-payment-method "Free Tier" has been deprecated and replaced by a "Developer Tier" that requires a payment method. This report provides a definitive, sourced analysis of the three core questions: registration requirements, whether a credit card is needed, and the specific rate limits imposed.

The critical finding is that SambaNova's documentation is internally contradictory on the credit card question. The official Rate Limits Policy states that a "Free Tier" applies when no payment method is linked, while the official Plans page states "Add a payment method and purchase credits to run your first requests." Community reports and third-party integrations confirm that new accounts created after the Developer Tier launch (February 8, 2025) require a payment method to make any API calls, effectively eliminating the truly free tier. The $5 free credit offered at launch may no longer be available for new sign-ups. Rate limits for accounts without a payment method are extremely restrictive at 20 requests per day per model, while the Developer Tier offers 60–240 requests per minute depending on the model.

16-## 2. Registration Requirements for the Free Tier
--
20:Creating a SambaNova Cloud account follows a straightforward process documented by both official sources and third-party walkthroughs. The user navigates to cloud.sambanova.ai, clicks "Sign Up," and provides an email address and password. After accepting the terms of service, the platform sends a verification email to the provided address. Once the email is verified, the user is redirected to the SambaCloud Dashboard [1].

22-### 2.2 Required and Optional Fields
23-
24:The core registration requires only an email address and a password. No phone verification is enforced at any stage of the sign-up process, which distinguishes SambaNova from some competitors that require phone verification for API access [1]. The platform does not require an organization name, use-case description, or other demographic fields during initial registration, though such information may be requested later during billing setup.

26-### 2.3 Social Login Options
27-
28:SambaNova Cloud supports multiple authentication methods beyond email-and-password. Google OAuth login is available and has been confirmed as a working authentication method through community support threads. A user who encountered a login error with Google was able to have the issue resolved by SambaNova support, confirming that Google login is a supported and maintained option [2]. Microsoft login is also available, though with a notable caveat: while personal Outlook accounts work successfully, Microsoft corporate or work accounts may encounter issues. A SambaNova support representative confirmed they tested Outlook login successfully but acknowledged that corporate Microsoft accounts could face authentication problems, and this issue was raised with the authentication development team [3].

30-### 2.4 Email Verification
31-
32-Email verification is a mandatory step in the registration process. The user must check their inbox and click a verification link before being granted access to the dashboard. The verification window is not explicitly documented, but the process is described as immediate in practice [1]. Once verified, the user can access the dashboard, explore the Playground, generate API keys, and view plans.
